## Hosted-service licensing

ELv2 restricts providing the software to third parties as a hosted or managed service when users receive access to a substantial set of its features or functionality. A provider seeking those rights must obtain a separate commercial agreement from the applicable rights holder.

Running an installation internally and offering a managed Agent Operations service to customers are different licensing cases. The exact license text controls; this document does not expand its restrictions.

Commercial terms, support, minimum commitments and any usage-based fees would be defined in that separate agreement.

For the proposed managed service, current capabilities, provider responsibilities and a way to discuss a technical pilot, see [Cloud providers](cloud-providers.md). No commercial license or support commitment is granted merely by opening a pilot inquiry.
